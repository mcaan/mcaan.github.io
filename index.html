01 / EXPERIMENTATIONA/B testing

Product notification experiment

Would a new category notification increase purchases without causing too many users to uninstall?

THE FINDING

Transactions rose 7.40 percentage points, while uninstalls rose 2.03 points. The recommendation: iterate on the notification before broad rollout.

Randomization checks · confidence intervals · guardrails · revenue impact
